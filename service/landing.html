How it works

Evidence for the process, not just the final file.

ProofOfProcess records the shape of collaboration: who acted, what changed, what was reviewed, what tools were used, and when each checkpoint existed.

01 / CAPTURE

Observe meaningful events

Human prompts, AI outputs, tool calls, approvals, revisions, artifact changes, and delegated actions are normalized into a common event protocol.

Capture-independent
02 / ATTEST

Cryptographically bind the record

Canonicalized events are hashed, sequenced, Merkle-committed, and signed with post-quantum attestation keys.

ML-DSA-65
03 / VERIFY

Prove selectively

Share a signed certificate that anyone can validate independently, against Bitcoin block headers, with no dependency on us.
